# EMBERFALL: THE HOLLOW CROWN
### A tactical fantasy adventure in twelve scenes, seven battles and one very small crown

*Shooting script for the game. Every spoken line in the game is in this document, word for word, and the build checks that. Lines marked* ⟶ *are branches; the bracketed notes after a choice say what it changes later.*

---

## THE CAST

| | Who | Voice direction |
|---|---|---|
| **THE NARRATOR** | You, more or less. Wry, warm, second person, faintly amused by your life choices. | A storyteller by a fire who has seen this go wrong before. Never shouts. Lands jokes by underplaying them. |
| **BRANNOC** | Oathsworn fighter. Huge, loud, kind. Names everything. Once ran from a battle and has never forgiven himself. | Big warm baritone, quick to laugh, quieter when it matters. |
| **WREN** | Shadow rogue. Sardonic, quick, owes money to the wrong man. Robbed a bishop once and will not shut up about it. | Dry, fast, low-key. Every line is a raised eyebrow. |
| **ISOLDE** | Ember wizard. Brilliant, precise, nervous. Burned down part of the Collegium. "It was a small fire." | Crisp, slightly formal, speeds up when excited. |
| **MAREN** | Dawn cleric. Gentle, serene, quietly terrifying. "Forgives" everyone in advance. | Soft, calm, kind — the threat lives in the calm. |
| **SKARN** | Gloomkin chief. Refers to himself in the third person. Owns two hats. | Fast, squeaky-proud, delighted with himself. |
| **GRATH** | Bridge troll. Has invented a toll. Not a bad guy, just bad at being a guy. | Slow, deep, rumbling, very sincere. |
| **MOTHER GRISK** | Swamp hag. Knits with hair. Sells secrets. Calls everyone "dearie". | Sweet old granny with something rotten under the sugar. |
| **PELL THE RAT** | Thornwick fence and loan shark. Wren's creditor. | Oily, friendly, smiling with too many teeth. |
| **BIG ODO** | Keeper of the Drowned Rat tavern. Enormous, jolly, knows everything. | Booming, cheerful, a laugh in every line. |
| **ILSA** | A "hostage" chained to the chapel gate. Ashen cultist. Terrible liar. Excellent letter writer. | Bright and breathy; snaps to sharp when caught. |
| **SIR OSSRIC** | Undead knight of the Bone Gate. Has kept the King's appointments for four hundred years. | Pompous, precise, offended by everything. Old-fashioned diction. |
| **THE HOLLOW KING** | A tyrant who refused to die. Born Aldric Vane, a very small king with a very large monologue. | Deep, slow, theatrical, enjoying every syllable — and easily interrupted. |

### Running jokes and payoffs (map)

| Setup | Where | Payoff |
|---|---|---|
| Skarn's OTHER hat | Opening | Loot item (+1 AC) → can be offered to Grath at the bridge → the King wants "your opinions as a hat" → Grath's hat club in the epilogue |
| The pay was a sandwich | Camp 1 | Odo's free mystery sandwich → if Maren trades her memory to Grisk, she forgets what a sandwich is → final line of the game |
| Brannoc names things | Camp 1 (sword "Margaret") | The hounds ("Biscuit" and "Lord Snuffles") → the King's scythe ("Gerald") in the camp bet → the King accepts it |
| Wren robbed a bishop | Opening / Camp 1 | The bishop is drinking in the Drowned Rat. He can be robbed again. |
| "It was a small fire" | Camp 1 | Grisk: "It wasn't a small fire." → Isolde burns Corvane's notes: "This one's a small fire." |
| Maren forgives in advance | Camp 2 | Grisk: she keeps a list → Epilogue: "forgiven retroactively" |
| Ilsa's angry letter | Chapel | If you leave her chained, her letter reaches the King mid-fight |
| Ilsa's fake brother | Odo's rumor in town | Lets you catch her lie at the chapel |
| Ossric's appointments | Bone Gate | Odo's jam "seal" → epilogue: Ossric is the Drowned Rat's doorman |
| The King's monologue | Crypt | He is interrupted every time he tries to finish it |
| Who hired the caravan? | Opening | Corvane's notes: "We were never the escort. We were the delivery." |

### Systems that the story drives

* **Approval.** Companions approve or disapprove of choices. Anyone at **+3 or higher** walks into the final battle **Inspired** (+1 to hit). Approval also decides each companion's epilogue.
* **Personal moments → perks.** Brannoc (camp 1): *Oath* (+1 Second Wind) or *Iron* (+1 AC). Wren (Thornwick): *Clean Slate* (+1 to hit). Isolde (chapel): *Corvane's Notes* (+1 Ember Burst, unlocks a binding at the boss) or *Clear Head* (+1 Arcane Darts). Maren (chapel bell): *Dawn* (+1 Cure Wounds).
* **Allies.** Grath and Ilsa can turn up in the final battle and fight beside you, depending on earlier choices.
* **The Hollow King has three phases.** At 60% he summons the dead (and, if you made friends, so do you). At 30% he starts tearing the sigils out of your hands, and what you learned on the road decides how you stop him.

# ACT ONE — THE ROAD

## SCENE 1. EXT. THE THORNWICK ROAD — NIGHT
*(Opening cinematic. Painted still: a burning caravan, a cracked black iron box in the mud. Music: low strings, embers. Title card fades.)*

**NARRATOR**
The caravan to Thornwick is burning. A moment ago, the black iron crown you were paid to guard split open with a scream. Now you are waking up in a ditch, an ember-sigil seared into your palm, three strangers groaning beside you. You feel… employed. Briefly.

**NARRATOR**
Somewhere, a horse is making the noise horses make when they have decided to quit. You can relate.

*(The four heroes sit up in the mud, one by one, each portrait sliding in as they speak.)*

**BRANNOC**
On your feet! Whatever that crown did to us can wait. We have company.

**WREN** *(peering round the wagon)*
Gloomkin raiders. Three of them, and a chief with a very ugly hat. They haven't spotted us behind the wagon yet.

**SKARN** *(off, hugely pleased with himself)*
OI! Crown-thieves! That shiny crown is Skarn's now. Skarn will wear it on Skarn's head, next to Skarn's OTHER hat!

**WREN**
He has two hats. On one head. I respect it and I hate it.

**ISOLDE** *(staring at her palm)*
These marks… they're warm. Like something is listening through them.

**BRANNOC**
Introductions! Quickly. Brannoc. Oathsworn. I hit things for good causes.

**WREN**
Wren. I take things for bad causes. Mostly mine.

**ISOLDE**
Isolde. Collegium of Embers. Formerly. There was a small fire.

**MAREN** *(serene, sincere, alarming)*
Maren, of the Dawnmother. Whatever happens next, I forgive you.

**WREN**
We haven't done anything yet.

**MAREN**
I know.

> **CHOICE —** MAREN: *"Now. How do we take them?"*
> 1. **"Wren — get us in close. Quietly."** *(Wren · Stealth DC 12)* — Wren approves · Brannoc disapproves
> 2. **"No tricks. CHARGE!"** — Brannoc approves · Wren disapproves
> 3. **"Brannoc, shove the burning wagon down the hill at them."** *(Brannoc · Athletics DC 13)* — Isolde approves

⟶ **Stealth succeeds** *[every hero gets advantage on their first attack]*

**NARRATOR**
You slip through the smoke like a rumor. Not one of them sees you coming. Every hero gets advantage on their first attack.

⟶ **Stealth fails**

**NARRATOR**
A burning wheel snaps beneath Wren's boot with the volume of a cathedral bell. Every goblin turns. So much for subtle.

⟶ **Charge**

**BRANNOC**
FOR… whatever this is!

⟶ **The wagon rolls** *[one goblin cutter is removed from the fight]*

**NARRATOR**
Brannoc puts his shoulder to the burning wagon and heaves. It rolls downhill, gathers speed and opinions, and flattens a goblin who had just finished saying "what's that noise". One fewer cutter.

⟶ **The wagon doesn't**

**NARRATOR**
The wagon does not move. Brannoc does, slightly, into the mud. The goblins watch this with real interest, then attack.

### BATTLE 1 — THE BURNING CARAVAN
*Skarn, two Gloomkin cutters, one Gloomkin archer. Burning wagons block lines of sight. In-combat barks only; Skarn taunts when hurt and when he drops. Tutorial prompts on screen, not voiced.*

*(Victory. Loot: **Skarn's Other Hat** — +1 AC. "It is still warm. Do not think about why." Everyone reaches level 2.)*

## SCENE 2. EXT. A HOLLOW OFF THE ROAD — NIGHT (CAMP)
*(Campfire scene. Low music. This is the "breathing" scene: exposition, then Brannoc's personal moment, then the sandwich.)*

**NARRATOR**
Night falls. You make camp in a hollow off the road. The fire crackles, and the sigil on your palm pulses in time with it, like a second heartbeat that does not belong to you.

**ISOLDE**
I studied the crown's shards while you three argued over the chief's hat. It belonged to the Hollow King, a tyrant who refused to die. Our marks are pieces of his crown. He wants them back.

**WREN**
And by "back" you mean…

**ISOLDE**
Out of our hands. Possibly with the hands still attached.

**MAREN**
Who hired the caravan? Someone knew what was in that box.

**ISOLDE** *(a beat too quickly)*
The contract was sealed by the Collegium. That's all I know. That's… all I'd like to know, actually.

*(SETUP: the Collegium. Pays off in Scene 7.)*

### Brannoc's moment

**NARRATOR**
Brannoc is sharpening his sword with the tenderness most people reserve for small animals.

**WREN**
Does it have a name? It has a name. Look at his face. It has a name.

**BRANNOC**
Margaret.

**WREN**
Your sword is called Margaret.

**BRANNOC**
It was my mother's name. She also hit things for good causes.

*(The joke lands; let it sit. Then the fire pops, and he stops smiling.)*

**BRANNOC**
I should tell you something, since we might die. At Greywater, twelve years ago, the line broke. I ran. Everyone who stayed is still there.

**BRANNOC**
I took the oath after. Swore I'd never run again. Some nights I'm not sure the oath took.

> **CHOICE —** BRANNOC: *"So. Now you know what you're following."*
> 1. **"Then swear it again. Here, with us as witnesses."** — Brannoc approves · Maren approves → *perk: Brannoc's Oath (+1 Second Wind)*
> 2. **"Running kept you alive. Next time, wear more armor so you don't have to."** — Wren approves → *perk: Iron Hide (+1 AC)*

⟶ **Swear it again**

**BRANNOC** *(standing, quietly enormous)*
Then hear it, all of you. I will not run. Not from goblins, not from kings, not from Maren when she's angry.

**MAREN**
That last one is unwise.

**NARRATOR**
Brannoc's Oath: Brannoc gets one more use of Second Wind.

⟶ **More armor**

**BRANNOC**
That is the most practical comfort anyone has ever given me. I hate how well it works.

**NARRATOR**
Iron Hide: Brannoc's armor class rises by one.

*(SETUP: "I ran once." Pays off in the final battle.)*

### The road ahead

> **CHOICE —** BRANNOC: *"The marks pull north: through the Sallow Mire, past Grath's Bridge, to Thornwick and the old chapel beyond. Before we sleep?"*
> 1. **"Search the wrecked wagons for supplies."** *(Isolde · Investigation DC 12)* — Wren approves
> 2. **"Maren, bless us for the road ahead."** — Maren approves → *the next battle starts Blessed (+2 to hit)*
> 3. **"Just sleep. We'll need it."** — Isolde approves

⟶ **Search succeeds** *(+2 Potions of Healing)*

**NARRATOR**
Beneath a scorched tarp you find two Potions of Healing, still corked, and a goblin's diary. The diary is mostly drawings of hats. (+2 Potions)

⟶ **Search fails**

**NARRATOR**
You find ash, a broken lute, and one very sad boot. You feel the boot's loneliness deeply.

⟶ **Bless**

**MAREN**
"Dawnmother, keep these fools alive. Mostly Brannoc — he needs it most." (Everyone is Blessed, +2 to hit, for the next fight.)

⟶ **Sleep** *(no line; straight to the banter)*

**WREN**
So. Cursed crown, undead king, mystery brands. Anyone else regret taking the escort job?

**BRANNOC**
The pay was good!

**WREN**
The pay was a sandwich, Brannoc.

**MAREN**
It was a good sandwich.

*(SETUP: the sandwich. Pays off at Odo's, and in the last line of the game.)*

**NARRATOR**
Long rest. You sleep like the dead, which is ironic, given where you are going. Health and abilities restored.

## SCENE 3. EXT. THE SALLOW MIRE — DAY
*(New area. Painted still: green-black water, drowned trees, drifting wisp-lights, a stilted hut.)*

**NARRATOR**
By noon the road sinks into the Sallow Mire, a swamp so damp it has opinions about your boots. Lights drift over the water. Wren says don't follow the lights. Brannoc is already following the lights.

**NARRATOR**
At the heart of the mire stands a hut on spindly stilts. On the porch, an old woman knits something long and grey. It is not wool.

**MOTHER GRISK** *(sweet as poisoned pie)*
Visitors! Come in, come in, dearies. Mind the toads. They're friendly. They're also hungry. Those aren't opposites.

**ISOLDE**
Is that… hair? Is she knitting with hair?

**MOTHER GRISK**
Waste not, dearie. Mother Grisk sells what folk keep hidden. Secrets. Names. The odd recipe. And you four are just stuffed with secrets.

**MOTHER GRISK**
Shall I show you? A free sample. Grisk is generous on Tuesdays.

**WREN**
It's not Tuesday.

**MOTHER GRISK**
It is in here, dearie.

*(She points her needle at each of them in turn. One secret each. Fast rhythm — each reveal, each reaction.)*

**MOTHER GRISK**
The big one cries at weddings. Every wedding. He's been to forty. Some of them he wasn't invited to.

**BRANNOC**
Love is beautiful and I will not apologize.

**MOTHER GRISK**
The little thief's real name is Wrenhilda.

**WREN**
Say that again and you'll be knitting with your own hair.

**MOTHER GRISK**
And the clever one. It wasn't a small fire, was it, dearie? East wing, west wing, the bell tower, and a very surprised goose.

**ISOLDE**
The goose was fine. Eventually.

**MOTHER GRISK**
And the priestess. She doesn't forgive any of you. She keeps a list.

**MAREN** *(not missing a beat)*
It's a short list. Brannoc is on it twice.

**BRANNOC**
TWICE?

**MOTHER GRISK**
Now. Business. The thing that's coming for your hands has a name. A true name, from before he was hollow. Say it to his face and he'll remember being small.

**MOTHER GRISK**
Grisk will trade it. For one memory. Just a little one. You won't even miss it.

> **CHOICE —** GRISK: *"Well, dearies? One memory, one name. Fair's fair."*
> 1. **"Maren, give her one memory. Something small."** — Isolde approves · Maren disapproves → *learns the true name · Maren loses a memory*
> 2. **"Isolde, take the name straight out of her head."** *(Isolde · Arcana DC 14)* — Isolde approves → *learns the true name on a success*
> 3. **"No deals with hags. Brannoc?"** — Brannoc approves · Maren approves
>
> *Every branch ends in a fight. Grisk was always going to eat you; the choice is what you leave with.*

⟶ **Maren's memory**

**MAREN**
Take the sandwich. The one from the caravan. I'll live.

**NARRATOR**
Grisk plucks something silver out of the air beside Maren's ear and pops it into a jar. Maren blinks, and looks faintly puzzled, like someone who has walked into a room and forgotten why.

**MOTHER GRISK**
Aldric Vane. That was his name, when he was a man, and a short one. Pleasure doing business! Now — supper.

⟶ **Isolde reads her (success)**

**NARRATOR**
Isolde's sigil flares. For half a heartbeat she is inside the hag's mind, which smells like boiled cabbage and spite. She comes out holding a name: Aldric Vane.

**MOTHER GRISK**
Thief! Grisk was going to be so nice about eating you, and now she won't be.

⟶ **Isolde reads her (failure)**

**MOTHER GRISK**
Naughty! Nobody reads Grisk without paying. Now Grisk reads YOU. With her teeth.

⟶ **No deals**

**BRANNOC**
Margaret says no.

**MOTHER GRISK**
Margaret? Oh, the sword. Charming. I'll knit you a sheath. From you.

# ACT TWO — THE TOWN

## SCENE 4. EXT. GRATH'S BRIDGE — DAWN

**NARRATOR**
At dawn you reach the river. A troll the size of a hay cart squats on the only bridge, two shadow-hounds snapping at his heels. He has the unmistakable look of a creature who has invented a fee.

**GRATH**
Bridge toll! Forty gold, or I grind yer bones fer bread. …No wait, that's me cousin. Forty gold or a fight.

**BRANNOC** *(delighted, pointing)*
Those hounds. That one's a Biscuit. And that one is definitely a Lord Snuffles.

**GRATH**
Their names are Gnash and Ruin.

**BRANNOC**
Not anymore.

> **CHOICE —** GRATH: *"Well? Grath is hungry and Grath is bad at waiting."*
> 1. **"Pay the toll."** *(40 gold; greyed out if you can't afford it)* — Isolde approves · Wren disapproves
> 2. **"Something worse than us is coming, Grath. Let us pass, and we'll deal with it."** *(Maren · Persuasion DC 14)* — Maren approves → *Grath becomes an ally*
> 3. **"Move. Or swim."** *(Brannoc · Intimidation DC 15)* — Brannoc approves
> 4. **"Offer Grath the hat."** *(only if you're carrying Skarn's Other Hat; it leaves your inventory)* — Wren approves · Brannoc approves → *Grath becomes an ally*
> 5. **"Attack!"** — Brannoc approves · Maren disapproves

⟶ **Pay**

**GRATH**
Pleasure doin' business! Mind the hounds, they bite tourists. And locals. And Grath.

⟶ **Persuade (success)**

**GRATH**
…The Hollow King? Grath hates that guy. Here, take this, and go break his stupid crown.

**GRATH**
If yer still alive later, Grath might come help. Grath likes a big fight. Grath does NOT like that guy.

⟶ **Intimidate (success)**

**NARRATOR**
Grath takes one look at Brannoc, yelps like a kettle, and dives into the river, abandoning his hoard. You feel taller. (+25 gold)

⟶ **The hat**

**NARRATOR**
Wren holds up Skarn's Other Hat. Grath goes very still. A single tear rolls down a face like a landslide.

**GRATH**
Is it… for Grath?

**WREN**
It is now.

**GRATH**
Nobody ever gives Grath a hat. Everybody just gives Grath a hard time. You come back if you need a troll. Grath is a good troll to have.

⟶ **Any check fails**

**GRATH**
Hah! Nice try, little meat. HOUNDS!

⟶ **Attack**

**BRANNOC**
Steel it is! Biscuit, Lord Snuffles — nothing personal.

## SCENE 5. INT. THE DROWNED RAT, THORNWICK — EVENING
*(New area. Painted still: a crowded low-beamed tavern, a hearth roaring, a stuffed rat in a tiny crown over the bar. Music: a jaunty fiddle, half-drowned in noise.)*

**NARRATOR**
Thornwick is a town built by people who wanted to be left alone, and then extended by people who wanted a drink. You find both at the Drowned Rat, which has a stuffed rat above the bar wearing a tiny crown. Nobody remembers why. Nobody asks.

**BIG ODO**
Welcome to the Drowned Rat! Boots off the tables, swords stay sheathed, and if you're here to fight, take it outside, or pay for the furniture first.

**WREN** *(low)*
Keep your heads down. If a man with a ledger smiles at me, we leave.

**NARRATOR**
A man with a ledger smiles at Wren.

**PELL THE RAT**
Wren! My favorite debtor. Sixty gold, plus interest, plus the interest on the interest, plus my feelings. Call it sixty. I'm sentimental.

**WREN**
Pell. You look exactly as rich as I remember.

**PELL THE RAT**
And you look like you're about to tell me you don't have it. Again. Boys?

**NARRATOR**
Two very large men stand up from a corner table. They do this slowly, the way mountains would, if mountains were paid by the hour.

**ISOLDE**
Is that a bishop asleep at the bar?

**WREN**
Don't look at the bishop.

*(PAYOFF: the bishop Wren robbed. SETUP: he can be robbed again.)*

> **CHOICE —** PELL: *"Sixty gold, little bird. Or we do this the Drowned Rat way."*
> 1. **"Pay her debt."** *(60 gold; greyed out if you can't afford it)* — Wren approves · Maren approves
> 2. **"Wren — lift his ledger. No ledger, no debt."** *(Wren · Sleight of Hand DC 13)* — Wren approves
> 3. **"Walk away, Pell. While you still can."** *(Brannoc · Intimidation DC 14)* — Brannoc approves
> 4. **"The Drowned Rat way it is."** — Brannoc approves · Maren disapproves → *tavern brawl*

⟶ **Pay**

**PELL THE RAT**
A pleasure, as always. Your friends are a bad influence on you, little bird. They're making you honest.

⟶ **Lift the ledger (success)**

**NARRATOR**
Wren bumps into Pell with an apology so sincere it should be framed. When she steps back, the ledger is under her coat, and Pell is patting his pockets like a man discovering he is in a story.

**PELL THE RAT**
Where's my… You little—

**BIG ODO**
No fighting in my tavern, Pell! Not when you're losing.

⟶ **Walk away (success)**

**NARRATOR**
Brannoc stands up to his full height, which takes a while. Pell does the math, and the math is bad.

**PELL THE RAT**
On reflection, the debt is… paused. Out of respect for your large friend.

⟶ **Any check fails**

**PELL THE RAT**
Oh, I was hoping you'd say something like that. Boys!

⟶ **Brawl (also after any failure)**

**BIG ODO**
BRAWL! Nobody draws a blade, or Odo draws his!

**NARRATOR**
House rules: fists, chairs and bottles. Anyone who drops is knocked out, not killed. Odo has a mop for the rest.

## SCENE 6. INT. THE DROWNED RAT — LATER

### Wren's moment

**NARRATOR**
Later, in a corner booth, Wren turns her page from Pell's ledger over in her hands. It has a lot of crossings-out. Most of them are in her handwriting.

**WREN**
You want to know what it was for. It's fine. Everyone does.

**WREN** *(flat, fast, like ripping off a bandage)*
My brother got sick. The healer wanted sixty gold up front. Pell lent it. My brother died anyway, two weeks later. The debt didn't.

**WREN**
So I ran. Robbed bishops. Didn't pay Pell. Told myself it was funny.

**WREN** *(a breath)*
It was a little funny. The bishop thing was very funny.

> **CHOICE —** WREN: *"So what do I do with this?"*
> 1. **"Burn it. You don't owe anybody for that."** — Wren approves · Maren approves → *perk: Clean Slate (Wren +1 to hit)*
> 2. **"Keep it. A page from Pell's ledger is leverage."** — Wren disapproves → *+30 gold, no perk*

⟶ **Burn it**

**WREN**
…Yeah. Yeah, okay.

**NARRATOR**
The page curls in the candle flame and is gone. Wren watches it the whole way. Afterwards she sits up a little straighter, like someone took a pack off her back that she had stopped noticing. Clean Slate: Wren gets plus one to hit.

⟶ **Keep it**

**WREN**
Leverage. Right. That's very… practical.

**NARRATOR**
Wren pockets the page. Later she sells the rest of Pell's secrets to a man in a hood for thirty gold. She doesn't smile when she counts it. (+30 gold)

### Odo's Emporium
*(A hub. The party can do these in any order, then leave. Each option disappears once used, except potions.)*

**BIG ODO**
Right! Odo's Emporium is open. That's the bar, but with prices. Potions, fifteen gold a bottle. Gossip is free. Gossip is always free.

> **CHOICE (repeats) —** ODO: *"Anything else, friends?"*
> * **"Buy a Potion of Healing."** *(15 gold, up to three)*
> * **"Heard anything about the old chapel?"** → *sets up Ilsa*
> * **"Anything about a knight under the chapel?"** → *gets the jam "seal"*
> * **"Wren. The bishop. Again."** — Wren approves · Maren disapproves → *+20 gold*
> * **"Time to go. The chapel's waiting."**

⟶ **The chapel**

**BIG ODO**
The chapel? Cult took it a month back. Black candles, chanting, the works. Oh — and there's a girl comes in sometimes, sits right there, practicing her brother's name.

**BIG ODO**
Out loud. Different name every time. Tuesday it was Tomas. Thursday it was Gregor. Lovely girl. Terrible liar.

⟶ **The knight**

**BIG ODO**
Sir Ossric? The old Bone Gate? My granddad's granddad tried to get past him. Ossric wanted an appointment. Granddad's granddad didn't have one. Granddad's granddad is still waiting.

**BIG ODO**
Tell you what. Ossric loves a seal. Take this — it's the lid off my jam jar. Very official, if you squint. And he's a skeleton, so he's always squinting.

⟶ **The bishop**

**NARRATOR**
The Bishop of Lower Thornwick is asleep in his ale. Wren relieves him of his purse with the gentle efficiency of a gardener picking a tomato. It is the second time she has done this. He does not seem to have learned anything. (+20 gold)

**MAREN**
I'm adding that to the list.

**WREN**
Which list?

**MAREN**
You know which list.

⟶ **Leave**

**BIG ODO**
One more thing. Here. Mystery sandwich, on the house. Don't ask what's in it. Odo doesn't know either, and Odo made it.

**BRANNOC**
The pay was a sandwich, and now the reward is a sandwich. We've come full circle.

*(+1 Potion of Healing. The sandwich heals exactly like a potion. Nobody questions this.)*

# ACT THREE — THE CHAPEL

## SCENE 7. EXT. THE CHAPEL OF THE DAWNMOTHER — DUSK

**NARRATOR**
The chapel of the Dawnmother sits cracked open on the hill, black candles guttering in every window. A young woman is chained to the gate. She looks remarkably well rested for a hostage.
